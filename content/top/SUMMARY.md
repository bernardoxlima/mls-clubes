# Top MLS clubs — acquisition recon (public sources)

Generated: 2026-08-27 (America/Sao_Paulo). Public web + Members Book 2026 + Festival aggregates (no PII from local CSV). Instagram bios from public indexes (Viralist) where the IG app is login-walled. No Apify. No login bypass.

Scope: Festival top-15 by `members_count` + ClaxClub (platinum flagship, rank 25 by members). Ranking source: `/workspace/mls-clubs/catalog/ranking-top25.json`. Book promises: `/workspace/mls-clubs/catalog/clubs.json`.

## 5-line pattern of how the winners acquire

1. **Personal brand is the TOFU, not the club site.** Founder IG (often 50k–300k+) + LinkedIn + YouTube/podcast dump traffic; the club account is a satellite (or empty). Keyword comments (`FIRE`, `Eu quero`, `META`, `VENDAS`, `Plano`) open DM → apply.
2. **Apply → qualify → closer, never open checkout for the MLS título.** Landing asks revenue, Instagram, WhatsApp, specialty; an “executivo” or consultant books a diagnostic/strategic call. Price of the league title is almost never public.
3. **Vertical high-ticket profession wins the Festival.** 9/15 are medicina/saúde (plus Black Diamond fisioterapia). FIRE is contábil/SERAC. Advogado 10X is jurídico. Makers and SHP sell *to mentors*. Nichos with private-pay, high ticket, and a “faculdade não ensinou vender” story.
4. **Gold/Platinum sell ambience + founder celebrity; Bronze that scales sells implementation.** Doc4U/Prosperus/ClaxClub/FIRE ride Flávio/Joel/Caio or a SERAC empire. Mentoria Makers (bronze, #1 members) sells *done-with-you funnels* plus MLS title. SHP sells active outbound (and brags it did it without ads).
5. **A cheap/mid front-end feeds the club.** Event/imersão/workshop/desafio/pós/YouTube aula gratuita → application for the annual mastermind. Endogin, Acelerador, SHP, Advogado 10X, CardioEasy, Walkyria RCA all run this two-step.

## What the best do that Bronze copies

| Winner move (Gold/Platinum + Makers) | How Bronze copies it | Gap the copy usually misses |
|---|---|---|
| Founder is already a category celebrity (Flávio on Doc4U, Joel on Prosperus, Caio on SHP, Jhonny “contador das estrelas”) | Club IG + comment-to-apply CTA | Celebrity co-mentor and earned media (Terra, Valor, PEGN) |

| MLS title + family/exec events as bonus, not the product | “Faça parte da MLS” on every LP | Real access to other clubs / Festival density |
| Front-end event with public ticket (Endogin, Acelerador, SHP workshop, Advogado Desafio) | Workshop / sessão de diagnóstico | Volume: Endogin/Acelerador run national calendars |
| Dual offer: grow the clinic *and* launch a mentoria | “crie seu produto digital” in book copy | SHP/Makers actually teach selling the mentoria |

Mentoria Makers is the exception that proves the rule: bronze by league tier, #1 by Festival members, because the commercial product is **implementation** (funis, páginas, anúncios, time comercial) rather than “3 encontros presenciais + WhatsApp”.

## Funnel archetypes seen on public pages

1. **Mastermind apply** (ClaxClub, Doc4U, Prosperus, Master Acelerador, EmpreendeDoc): LP → form → VIP/executivo call. Optional IG comment keyword.
2. **Diagnostic session** (Mentoria Makers, Z Scale/Leandro Reche, GL Club sessão estratégica): “reunião estratégica / sessão de diagnóstico” as the named CTA, then 12-month mentoria.
3. **Event → club** (Endogin curso R$11k–36k; SHP workshop gratuito; Acelerador YouTube + eventos; CardioEasy eventos 15k médicos; Advogado 10X Desafio/Hotmart).
4. **Comment-only, thin web** (FIRE Club; Next Level). No dedicated club LP found. Acquisition is founder social + “comente FIRE”.
5. **Public checkout** (rare for the MLS club): GL Club lists **R$ 25.000**. Walkyria/Endogin public prices are *front-end education*, not the league title.

## What they do **not** deliver (public vs book)

Labeled INFERENCE unless the site itself says it.

- **The MLS título price and liquidity** are not sold on the page. Book talks “título / patrimônio”; LPs use it as social proof, not a product spec.
- **Celebrity 1:1 is bait, group is the meal.** Book: Flávio “encontros regulares” (Doc4U), Joel as Prosperus mentor, Caio as SHP sócio. Sites promise “acompanhamento de Davison e Flávio” / “liderado por Dani e Joel”. They do **not** promise weekly 1:1 with the celebrity. INFERENCE: closer uses the name; delivery is group + staff.
- **Not a clinical course (most medical clubs).** Doc4U / Master / EmpreendeDoc / Doctor 360 / GL sell vendas-gestão-liderança. Endogin and MentorEasy/CardioEasy **do** sell clinical CME as the front-end; the MLS club is the business layer.
- **SHP explicitly is not ads/launch.** LinkedIn + workshop: scale without “milhares em lançamentos, tráfego pago ou milhões de seguidores”. Opposite of Makers (who *implement* ads).
- **FIRE does not have a public productized funnel.** No club LP found. Book promises 3 presenciais, resorts, family; web is SERAC personal brand + “comente FIRE”.
- **Bronze Makers does not sell “just ambience”.** Site and book match: they implement funis. What they don’t sell on the LP is a public price or a clinical nicho.
- **Next Level** (bronze, new in 2026) has identity/podcast, not a visible apply LP. Weakest public funnel in the top 15.

## Implications for an AI club with MIT-lab positioning

- Copy the **apply + diagnostic + closer** skeleton and a **named front-end** (workshop / lab day / VSL), not open checkout.
- Winners in Festival volume are **vertical** (médico, contador, advogado, fisio) or **meta** (teach mentors to sell). A generic “AI for founders” competes with Founders AI / NovaIA (already in MLS) unless the nicho is as sharp as Endogin (emagrecimento+TRH) or FIRE (contábil).
- Gold density at the Festival is **health + celebrity co-mentor**. Bronze that out-members Gold (Makers) sells **implementation**. An AI club that only sells “encontros + WhatsApp” will look like Next Level, not like Makers or Doc4U.
- Do **not** publish the título price if the rest of the league hides it; do publish a **revenue floor** (Makers R$30k/mês, Master R$500k/mês, Prosperus R$10M/ano) — that is how they signal ticket.

## Method / limits

- FACT = on a public page, Viralist IG index, LinkedIn company/about, or the Members Book.
- INFERENCE = pattern across pages, not a quote.
- UNKNOWN = not found on public HTML without login.
- Instagram.com returned HTTP 200 for many unindexed handles; only Viralist-confirmed or press/LinkedIn-cited handles are treated as FACT. Club handles that 404 on Viralist are marked UNKNOWN even if instagram.com 200s (login wall).
- No CPF / personal email / phone from `lista-evento-mls.csv`. Public site phones omitted.
