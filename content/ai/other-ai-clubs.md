## 9. Gaps vs a MIT-lab positioned club (whole league)

**FACT from everything above:**
1. **Nobody in MLS publicly claims MIT, a university lab, or “first AI lab in Latin America”.** That story is currently unoccupied.
2. The two real AI clubs (FoundersAI, NovaIA) both sell **implementation inside existing companies**, explicitly against courses, PPT, and tool-of-the-week.
3. Both hide the título sticker; NovaIA at least publishes ICP filters (R$ 3M revenue, ~R$ 120k investment readiness). FoundersAI publishes neither price nor faturamento mínimo.
4. Funnels are founder-LinkedIn + application + closer. Only the *cheap* FoundersAI adjacent product (Vibe Builders) has a public checkout.
5. Vertical AI (cyber, health, B2G, infoproducts) exists as a sprinkle, not as a general AI-lab peer group.

**INFERENCE — white space for Bernardo:**
- Academic / MIT / lab legitimacy
- Builder-researcher ICP (people who want to *create* AI systems / a lab, not only install ChatGPT automations)
- Latin America / Brazil-as-hub narrative
- Transparent research output, methods, or a named lab (none of the clubs publish this)
- A public, premium application that feels like a lab admissions process rather than a mentorship sales form

**Risk:** FoundersAI already owns “AI-First operator, 12 months, CEO cascade, agents, cash result” and has the stronger founder brand (Gama exit, Forbes, 38k IG). NovaIA owns “monthly AI project + business-model lens for R$3M+ SMEs”. Do not out-NovaIA NovaIA or out-Gui Gui on implementation. Outflank on **lab / MIT / research-grade + LatAm first**.
